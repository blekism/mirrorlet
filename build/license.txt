Mirrorlet - iPhone screen mirroring for Windows

Mirrorlet bundles UxPlay (https://github.com/FDH2/UxPlay), an open-source
AirPlay mirroring server, together with GStreamer, FFmpeg (libav) and other
open-source libraries built by the MSYS2 project.
