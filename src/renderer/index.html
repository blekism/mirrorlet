Connect your iPhone

  1. Join the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring (two overlapping rectangles).
  4. Choose Mirrorlet PC.

To stop, open Screen Mirroring again and tap Stop Mirroring. The mirror window turns sideways when you rotate the phone in apps that support landscape.
